Pick a coach

Three parody head coaches. Each knows certain styles: pace and space, run and gun, grit and grind, triangle, iso ball or switch everything.

Design the scheme, then draft

Set your style, defense and sliders. Every pick, the reels land on a team and a decade; draft anyone from that roster into an open spot. Teammates from the same squad add chemistry.

Play the season

82 games against made-up teams, then the playoffs. Watch them on the court or sim ahead. In clutch time you call the play. The analyst tells you which pick cost you wins.

Help

Are these real players and teams?

No. Clutch is a parody. Players, coaches and teams have fictional or joke names and are not affiliated with any real person, team, league or game publisher. Stats are our own invention.

Does it cost anything?

No. Clutch is free with ads: an occasional full-screen ad between seasons, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for an extra re-roll. There are no in-app purchases, coins or packs.

Do I need an account?

No. Your run in progress and your record book are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases them.

Can I share my Daily result?

Everyone gets the same Daily draft and season each day, so compare records with your friends.

How do I contact you?
