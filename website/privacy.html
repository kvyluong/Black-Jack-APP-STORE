In short: there are no accounts, and we don’t run servers that store information about you. Your chips and progress stay on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects device data to show and measure those ads. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

This policy covers the Blackjack Coach mobile app for iPhone and Android (the “app”) and this website, including the browser version of the game (the “site”). “We” means REPLACE_WITH_DEVELOPER_NAME, the developer of Blackjack Coach.

Information stored on your device

Your settings, play chips, levels, lesson progress, practice statistics and the result of the “Remove ads” purchase are saved on your device (in the browser’s local storage for the browser version). They are not sent to us. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner, ads between hands, and optional videos you can choose to watch for bonus play chips. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information such as:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data

Google processes this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, the app only uses your advertising identifier if you allow tracking when asked; you can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. If you buy “Remove ads”, the banner and the ads between hands stop; the optional bonus videos remain available but are only shown when you choose to watch one.

The browser version on this site shows no ads.

In-app purchases

The optional “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. The store tells the app that the purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on your other devices. Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies apply to the payment.

Spoken counts

Some practice exercises read card counts aloud using your device’s built-in text-to-speech. The app does not record audio and does not request microphone access.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • We don’t use analytics or crash-reporting services. (If we add one, we’ll update this policy first.)
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • The site loads no third-party fonts, scripts or trackers and sets no cookies.

Website hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to operate the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Blackjack Coach includes simulated gambling and is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we don’t store personal information on our own servers, most requests are best handled by clearing the app’s data on your device and by using the ad choices above. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can also contact Google directly. We are happy to help with any request: contact us using the address below.
