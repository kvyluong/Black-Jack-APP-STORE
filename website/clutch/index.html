One run, about five minutes

A fresh random draft every time, or the same Daily draft as everyone else.

Pick a coach

Three parody head coaches. Each knows certain formations and styles: high press, tiki-taka, counter-attack, park the bus, long ball or wing play.

Design the scheme, then draft

Set your formation, style and sliders. For each position the era dial picks a decade; draft one of five players. Same-nation neighbours add chemistry.

Play the season

38 matches against made-up clubs. Watch them on the board (fouls, cards, penalties and all) or sim ahead. The analyst tells you which pick cost you wins.

Help

Are these real players and clubs?

No. Clutch is a parody. Players, coaches and clubs have fictional or joke names and are not affiliated with any real person, club, league or game publisher. Stats are our own invention.

Does it cost anything?

No. Clutch is free with ads: an occasional full-screen ad between seasons, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for an extra era change. There are no in-app purchases, coins or packs.

Do I need an account?

No. Your run in progress and your record book are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases them.

Can I share my Daily result?

Everyone gets the same Daily draft and season each day, so compare records with your friends.
