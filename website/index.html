Questions

Is this gambling?

No. Blackjack Coach uses play chips only. Chips can’t be bought, cashed out or exchanged for anything. It’s a training tool for entertainment and education.

Is card counting legal?

Counting cards in your head is legal, but casinos are private businesses and may ask a player they suspect of counting to stop playing blackjack or to leave.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free with ads. An optional one-time purchase removes the ads. The browser version has no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, and your chips and progress are saved only on your device (or in your browser).

Which rules does it use?

By default, six decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split and late surrender, with blackjack paying 3:2. You can change decks, soft 17, double after split, surrender and the payout in Settings, and the coach and chart follow your rules.
