Help

Is there any betting or real money?

No. There are no chips, bets, prizes or cash-outs. The pot and bet in each question are numbers for the math problem. PotSharp is a training tool for education and entertainment.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free with ads between sets. An optional one-time purchase, Remove ads, turns them off.

How do I restore Remove ads on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open PotSharp, go to Settings and tap Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds follow Apple’s policy: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you have trouble, email us and we’ll help.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, and your progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app erases it.

How do I unlock the 60-second sprint?

Reach Level 3. Today’s 10 and one more set after the intro usually gets you there.

Can I use PotSharp during a real game?

No. It deals its own practice hands and has no way to enter real cards. It teaches the math so you can do it in your head.
