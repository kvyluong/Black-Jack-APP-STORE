Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: there are no accounts, and we don’t run servers that store information about you. Your progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects device data to show and measure those ads. Purchases are handled by Apple.

This policy covers the PotSharp mobile app for iPhone (the “app”) and this page. “We” means 9in1shampoo (Kevin Luong), the developer of PotSharp.

Information stored on your device

Your levels, XP, day streak, practice statistics, “EV won” score and the result of the “Remove ads” purchase are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: full-screen ads between sets of hands. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information such as:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Apple IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data

Google processes this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. The app only uses your advertising identifier if you allow tracking when asked; you can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you buy “Remove ads”, the ads between sets stop and the app shows no ads.

In-app purchases

The optional “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Apple (App Store). We never receive your payment details. The App Store tells the app that the purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on your other devices. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the payment.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • We don’t use analytics or crash-reporting services. (If we add one, we’ll update this policy first.)
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • This page loads no third-party fonts, scripts or trackers and sets no cookies.

Website hosting

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to operate the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

PotSharp teaches the math of a card game played in casinos and is intended for adults. It has no real-money gambling, betting, chips or prizes, and its “EV won” score can never be bought or cashed out. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we don’t store personal information on our own servers, most requests are best handled by clearing the app’s data on your device and by using the ad choices above. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can also contact Google directly. We are happy to help with any request: contact us using the address below.

Delete your data

We don’t have an account or a copy of your data to delete, so you control it on your device:

  • Progress: in the app, go to Settings → Reset progress. Deleting the app also erases everything it saved.
  • Advertising data: turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and see Google’s My Ad Center for data Google holds.
  • Purchase record: the “Remove ads” purchase is kept by Apple with your Apple Account.

If you want help or confirmation, email kvy.luong@gmail.com and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
