# App ideas

Future apps that reuse the ShoeSharp formula: a niche skill trainer people search for, free with AdMob ads, built on the same Expo + EAS setup.

## Next up: Poker odds trainer (logged 2026-11-19)

**Pitch:** ShoeSharp for poker. It trains the math, not gambling.

**Why poker first**
- After blackjack, it's the biggest casino-skill search market on the App Store.
- Same audience as ShoeSharp, so each app can promote the other for free (a "More from us" row, a home-screen tile).
- Reuses the ShoeSharp code: the card components, felt-table UI, XP/levels/streaks, drills, AdMob, the Remove ads purchase, and Spanish strings.

**Possible features**
- Pot odds and equity drills: "You need X% equity. Call or fold?"
- Counting outs: show a board and ask how many outs you have.
- Preflop range trainer by position (open, call, 3-bet).
- Rule of 2 and 4 quick-math drills.
- A daily challenge, streaks, and a "Table-ready" score like ShoeSharp's "Casino-ready".

**Before building**
- Check App Store competitors: names, ratings, pricing, and keyword gaps.
- Pick a name and icon that match ShoeSharp's brand.
- Keep it gambling-free for App Review: no real money and no chips for sale. It's a trainer only, like ShoeSharp.
