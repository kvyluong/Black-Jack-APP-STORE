# Reply to App Review: Guideline 2.1, Information Needed (1.0.0)

Apple asked (2026-10-22) for a screen recording on a physical device plus the answers below,
in a reply **and** in App Review Information → Notes. Paste the text under "Reply" into the
reply box, attach the recording, and paste the same text (without the recording line) into Notes.

## Recording checklist (physical iPhone, latest iOS)

Start recording from Control Center, then:

1. Launch ShoeSharp from the Home Screen (the recording must start with the launch).
2. First run: the tracking explainer and Apple's prompt, then the level picker ("I'm new").
3. Home: today's goals and the "Next step" card. Open a lesson and go through a page or two.
4. Play tab → Main Floor: set a bet, Deal, play a hand (Hit/Stand), show the coach's feedback, Next hand.
5. Practice tab: open the Strategy Drill and answer a few questions.
6. Me tab → Settings → **Remove ads**: show the App Store purchase sheet (sandbox; you can cancel),
   then **Restore purchase**.
7. Me tab: progress and stats. Stop recording.

Aim for 2–3 minutes. Keep the phone's notifications quiet (Focus) so nothing personal shows.

## Reply

Hello App Review team, thank you for the review. Here is the information you asked for. A screen
recording from a physical iPhone is attached.

**1. Screen recording:** attached. It starts with launching the app and shows the first-run
screens, a lesson, a hand at the practice table with the coach's feedback, a drill, and the
Remove ads purchase and Restore purchase on the Settings screen. The app has no accounts, no
login and no user-generated content.

**2. Purpose and audience:** ShoeSharp teaches adults (18+) to play blackjack correctly and to
count cards, as a skill and for fun. Players learn basic strategy through short lessons, practice
at a simulated table where a coach grades every decision and explains the right play, and train
with drills (strategy, running count, true count). It solves the problem that most blackjack apps
are casino games that never teach; ShoeSharp explains why each play is right. There is no
real-money gambling: chips are virtual, cannot be bought, won as prizes, or cashed out, and have
no monetary value.

**3. Setup and main features:** no setup, login or sample files are needed. On first launch the
player picks a starting level. Main features: Home tab (daily goals and the next lesson), Play
tab (practice tables), Practice tab (lessons and drills), Me tab (progress, stats, Settings).

**4. External services:** Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for ads, with Google's User
Messaging Platform for consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit)
for the one purchase; Apple's local notifications for an optional daily practice reminder. No
backend server, analytics account, payment processor or AI service. Progress is stored only on
the device.

**5. Regional differences:** the app works the same everywhere it is offered, in English and
Spanish. The only difference is the consent message, which Google shows in the EEA, UK and
Switzerland. The app is not offered in mainland China or South Korea.

**6. Regulated industry and third-party material:** ShoeSharp is not a gambling service. It
offers no real-money wagering, prizes or cash-out, so no gaming licence applies; the age rating
is 18+ for simulated gambling. Card counting is taught as a skill practiced on the app's own
virtual shoe; the app does not connect to or assist with play at real casinos. All content is
original; no protected third-party material is used.

**7. In-App Purchase:** one non-consumable, "Remove ads" (USD 2.99), which removes the
full-screen ads shown between hands. Optional reward videos for bonus virtual chips stay
available. To reach it: Me tab → Settings → Remove ads. Restore purchase is on the same screen.
