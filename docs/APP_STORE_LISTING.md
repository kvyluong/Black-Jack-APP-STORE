# ShoeSharp: App Store listing (search-optimized)

Paste these into App Store Connect. Character counts are checked against Apple's limits.

## How App Store search works (why the text is written this way)
- Apple ranks on the **app name** (strongest), then the **subtitle**, then the hidden
  **keyword field**. The description is **not** used for search ranking, but it
  converts visitors.
- Words are combined across all three. "blackjack" in the name plus "counting" in the
  subtitle already ranks for "blackjack counting". So **never repeat a word** across
  them: it wastes characters.
- Keywords: comma-separated, **no spaces**, singular forms (Apple matches plurals), no
  competitor names, no "free" (it adds nothing) and no category name.
- **Two localizations, two keyword fields.** The US App Store also indexes the
  **Spanish (Mexico)** listing. Adding it gives a second 100-character field and reaches
  Spanish speakers in the US and Latin America. The app is already fully translated.
- After launch, **ratings and download speed** drive rank, so the rating prompt matters.
  Revisit keywords every 4–6 weeks, based on App Store Connect → App Analytics → Sources.

## Other App Store Connect fields
- **Primary category:** Games → Card (best fit for searchers; strong for "blackjack"). **Secondary:** Education.
- **Age rating:** Simulated Gambling = Frequent/Intense → 17+.
- **Support / marketing URL:** the website, ideally on the custom domain (see APP_STORE_LAUNCH.md §4).
- **In-app purchase display name:** "Remove ads" / "Quitar anuncios". In-app purchases are also indexed by search and can show on the product page.

## After launch
- Add the Smart App Banner to the website `<head>` once the app has an App Store ID:
  `<meta name="apple-itunes-app" content="app-id=YOUR_APP_ID">`
- Track which search terms bring downloads (App Analytics → Sources → App Store Search).
  Swap out keywords that bring nothing.
- Ask happy players for ratings (the in-app rating prompt). Ratings count heavily in rank.
