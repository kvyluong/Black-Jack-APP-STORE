# Short: "Why the house always wins at blackjack"

**PRODUCT.** ShoeSharp: Blackjack Trainer (iOS and Android). A free trainer: a coach grades every
decision, with lessons and drills. Virtual chips only, no real-money gambling.

**STORY.** One idea per beat, each scene turning into the next:
1. Hook: "Why does the house ALWAYS win at blackjack?"
2. "It's not luck. It's who plays first." Your hand (10, 6) takes a King: BUST 26. Your chips slide away.
3. "Bust, and you lose on the spot, even if the dealer busts after." The dealer's hole card flips: 16, then
   a 6 makes 22. Dealer bust... too late.
4. "The dealer busts about 28% of the time." A counter runs from 0 to 28%.
5. House edge meter: a typical player gives up ~2% or more; perfect basic strategy cuts it to ~0.5%.
6. "Avoid 6:5 tables: +1.4% for the house." The meter jumps, red.
7. "Learn the right play for every hand." The strategy grid fills in, cell by cell.
8. End card: real app footage on a phone, ShoeSharp, "Free practice. No real money.", "Coming soon on
   iPhone & Android".

**FORMAT.** 1080×1920, 30 fps, 50 s, silent (music is added in the YouTube or TikTok app, so it can be a
trending sound). Captions carry everything, so it works with the sound off.

**LOOK.** Felt `#0B5D3B` / `#127A4F` / `#062F1F`, cream `#F4F1E8`, gold `#E8C547`, loss red `#FF6B6B`,
win green `#5BE38F`. SF Pro Display, weights 700–800. Cards are white with real suits, a soft shadow and
gold-rimmed chips.

**MOTION.** The camera never sits still (a slow push-in plus a drifting light). Words rise in one by one on
springs. Cards deal in with a short arc and a slight rotation. Numbers count up. The meter eases. Every
scene hands off to the next (the bust chips slide into the meter, the meter collapses into the grid,
the grid recedes behind the phone).

**SOUND.** None in the file.

**QUALITY CHECK.** Render, look at a frame every 0.5 s, critique it like a senior motion designer (legibility
at phone size, safe areas: nothing important in the top 220 px or bottom 380 px where Shorts puts its UI,
timing, overlaps), fix, and repeat.

**RULES.** Accurate numbers only (6-deck, S17, 3:2: basic strategy ≈0.5%; typical player 2%+; 6:5 adds
≈1.4%; dealer bust ≈28%). No real-money claims, no "beat the casino", and no store badges until the app
is live.
